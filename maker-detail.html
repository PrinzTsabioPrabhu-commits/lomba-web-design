Maker detail

Siti Rahma

Batik artisan • Pekalongan

Her story

Learning from the people before her

Siti Rahma grew up around batik workshops in Pekalongan, where every table, wax pot, and dye tub held a lesson. She began by observing her grandmother, and later by repeating the same hand motions until they felt like memory itself.

Her journey

Timeline

  • 2008 — Started learning batik from her grandmother.
  • 2012 — Created her first independent collection.
  • 2018 — Started teaching younger artisans in the neighborhood.
  • 2026 — Continuing the tradition with new experimental patterns.
“The cloth is only part of the story. The real value is in the knowledge passed from hand to hand.”
Her philosophy

Her craft

For Siti, batik is not a product for display alone. It is a conversation between time, technique, and identity. Each pattern keeps the memory of a place, and each collection becomes a bridge between generations.
